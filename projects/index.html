Projects

Our research develops physics-based computational methods for complex gas-dynamic and plasma phenomena, with an emphasis on kinetic theory, particle simulation, GPU-accelerated computing, and high-performance computing.

RESEARCH PORTFOLIO

Physics-Based Computational Science

We develop scalable simulation methods that connect fundamental nonequilibrium physics with challenging problems in plasma processing, semiconductor manufacturing, hypersonic aerothermodynamics, and scientific computing.

01
KINETIC SIMULATION

Comet

GPU-Accelerated Kinetic Simulation

A GPU-accelerated kinetic simulation framework for large-scale particle-based modeling of plasma and gas-dynamic systems.

Comet focuses on scalable particle simulation methods for predictive modeling of complex nonequilibrium phenomena on modern high-performance computing systems.

Research Focus Particle-in-Cell Direct Simulation Monte Carlo GPU Acceleration Unstructured Meshes Large-Scale Computing
02
SCIENTIFIC AI

CometAI

AI-Assisted Scientific Computing

An AI-enabled workflow for assisting the development, configuration, execution, and analysis of complex kinetic simulations.

CometAI combines scientific simulation with artificial intelligence to automate computational workflows while maintaining physics-based modeling and high-fidelity simulation capabilities.

Research Focus Scientific AI Workflow Automation Retrieval-Augmented Workflows Surrogate Modeling High-Performance Computing
03
LOW-TEMPERATURE PLASMA

Plasma-Enabled Manufacturing

Kinetic Modeling for Semiconductor Processing

Kinetic simulation of low-temperature plasmas used in advanced semiconductor manufacturing, with emphasis on plasma transport and plasma-surface interactions.

The research examines the ion and electron distributions that control plasma processing, including high-aspect-ratio etching and ion energy and angular distributions.

Research Focus RF Plasma Plasma Transport Plasma-Surface Interactions Semiconductor Etching Ion Energy Distributions
04
HYPERSONIC FLOW

Hypersonic Nonequilibrium Flow

Kinetic Modeling of Hypersonic Aerothermodynamics

Particle-based modeling of rarefied and nonequilibrium flows encountered in high-speed atmospheric entry and other extreme aerodynamic environments.

The research focuses on shock-layer physics, high-temperature nonequilibrium, and predictive modeling of complex hypersonic aerothermodynamic phenomena.

Research Focus Rarefied Gas Dynamics Nonequilibrium Flow Shock-Layer Physics Particle Simulation Aerothermodynamics Radiative Heating
05
HIGH-PERFORMANCE COMPUTING

GPU-Accelerated Particle Simulation

Scalable Algorithms and Numerical Methods

Development of computational algorithms that enable large-scale particle simulations on modern GPU-based high-performance computing systems.

Particular emphasis is placed on scalable numerical methods for unstructured meshes, particle-field coupling, and efficient solution of the field equations required by kinetic particle simulations.

Research Focus GPU Algorithms Unstructured-Mesh Simulation Particle-Field Coupling Charge Conservation Poisson Solvers Adaptive Mesh Refinement
RESEARCH VISION

From Kinetic Physics to Scalable Simulation

Across these projects, we develop a common computational foundation based on kinetic theory, particle simulation, and GPU-accelerated high-performance computing.

The long-term goal is to build predictive simulation capabilities that connect fundamental nonequilibrium physics with challenging problems in plasma processing, semiconductor manufacturing, hypersonic aerothermodynamics, and advanced scientific computing.
