## Low-Temperature Plasma

Kinetic modeling of low-temperature plasmas, plasma transport, plasma-surface interactions, and plasma-enabled manufacturing.

## Hypersonic Nonequilibrium Flow

Modeling of hypersonic flows, rarefied gas dynamics, nonequilibrium chemistry, and plasma effects using continuum and kinetic methods.

## Kinetic and Particle Methods

Development of particle-in-cell, direct simulation Monte Carlo, and coupled kinetic methods on unstructured meshes.

## GPU-Accelerated Scientific Computing

Development of scalable computational methods for large-scale scientific simulations using GPU-accelerated high-performance computing.
