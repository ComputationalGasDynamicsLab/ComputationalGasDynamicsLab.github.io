<h2>Thermal-Fluid Sciences for Aerospace and Mechanical Engineering</h2>

to analyze aerodynamic flows and develop physics-based engineering models.

<h3>Aerodynamics</h3>

<p>
Fundamentals of aerodynamic flows, including inviscid, incompressible, and
compressible flow; airfoils and wings; and shock and expansion waves.
</p>

</div>

<h3>Computational Fluid Dynamics</h3>

<p>
An introduction to the numerical solution of fluid-dynamics equations, with
emphasis on discretization, stability, and model problems for incompressible
and compressible flows.
</p>
