## Ph.D. Positions

I am looking for Ph.D. students to join my research group in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of North Dakota, starting Spring 2027 or Fall 2027. Students will work on recently funded research projects in either (1) low-temperature plasma or (2) hypersonic non-equilibrium flow. The projects focus on (1) understanding complex gas dynamics or plasma physics phenomena; (2) using GPU-accelerated high-performance computing; (3) developing next-generation computational methods based on kinetic theory, including the direct simulation Monte Carlo and particle-in-cell methods.

Applicants should have a B.S. or M.S. degree in Aerospace Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Materials Science and Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Computer Science, Chemistry, Physics, or a related field. To apply, please email chonglin.zhang@und.edu with the subject line "Ph.D. Application" and include a cover letter, CV, short research statement and interest, and unofficial transcripts as a single PDF.
