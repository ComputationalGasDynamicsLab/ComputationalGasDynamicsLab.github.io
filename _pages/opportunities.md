<h2>Funded Ph.D. Positions</h2>

<p>
I am looking for Ph.D. students to join my research group in the Department of
Mechanical Engineering at the University of North Dakota, starting <strong>Spring

projects.
</p>

<div class="opportunities-grid">

<h3>Low-Temperature Plasma</h3>

<p>
Study complex plasma physics phenomena through physics-based computational
methods for low-temperature plasma systems.
</p>

</div>

<div class="opportunity-card">

<div class="opportunity-number">02</div>

<h3>Hypersonic Nonequilibrium Flow</h3>

<p>
Study complex gas-dynamics phenomena in hypersonic nonequilibrium flows using
predictive particle-based simulations.
</p>

</div>

</div>

<div class="opportunities-methods">

<div>
<div class="opportunities-section-label">COMPUTATIONAL APPROACH</div>
<h2>Kinetic Theory to GPU-Accelerated Computing</h2>
</div>

<p>
The projects emphasize next-generation computational methods based on
<strong>kinetic theory</strong>, including <strong>Direct Simulation Monte Carlo</strong>
and <strong>Particle-in-Cell</strong>, and the use of
<strong>GPU-accelerated high-performance computing</strong>.
</p>

</div>
