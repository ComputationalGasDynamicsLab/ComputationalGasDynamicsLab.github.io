**Alseny** completed a summer research visit at the **Scientific Computation Research Center (SCOREC), Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute (RPI)** from **June 22 to July 24, 2026**, supported by the **NSF EPSCoR Research Fellows award**.

Hosted by **Prof. Mark Shephard and Jacob Merson**, the visit focused on **Comet code development, with an emphasis on the particle-in-cell (PIC) method for low-temperature plasma simulation**, advancing scalable computational approaches for high-performance computing.
