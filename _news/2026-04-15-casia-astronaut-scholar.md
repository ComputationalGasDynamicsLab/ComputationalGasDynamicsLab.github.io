Congratulations to **Casia Steinhaus**, who has been named the **first Astronaut Scholar from the University of North Dakota**. Casia is a rising senior majoring in **Physics and Mathematics**.

As an Astronaut Scholar, Casia will receive financial support, professional development opportunities, mentorship, and opportunities to represent UND at the **Astronaut Scholar Technical Conference**. We are incredibly proud of Casia and her outstanding achievements in undergraduate research. Congratulations, Casia!
