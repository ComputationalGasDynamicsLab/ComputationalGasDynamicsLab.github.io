Graduate students **Alseny Diallo** and **Wei Guo** attended the **5th US Low Temperature Plasma Summer School**, held **June 15–19, 2026**, at **North Carolina State University in Raleigh, North Carolina**, with travel support from the **North Dakota NASA EPSCoR Travel Grant**.

The summer school provided opportunities to learn about fundamental and emerging topics in low-temperature plasma science and to engage with researchers and students in the broader plasma community.
