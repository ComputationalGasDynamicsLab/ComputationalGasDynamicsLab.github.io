The Computational Gas Dynamics and Plasma Physics Lab received a **$299,677 award from the National Science Foundation (NSF)** through the **EPSCoR Research Fellows** program to advance GPU-accelerated kinetic modeling of low-temperature plasma.

The project will develop scalable particle simulation methods and apply them to plasma physics and advanced manufacturing.

[View the NSF award](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/show-award?AWD_ID=2531816).
