The Computational Gas Dynamics and Plasma Physics Lab received funding from the **National Science Foundation (NSF)** through the **EPSCoR Research Fellows** program to advance GPU-accelerated kinetic modeling of low-temperature plasma.

The project will develop scalable particle simulation methods for **electric space propulsion and microelectronics manufacturing**.
